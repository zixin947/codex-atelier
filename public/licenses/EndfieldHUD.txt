Source revision: 19de06a45a6fb701cac1be4a1571111a8813b798

Adapted in Atelier: surface and accent gradients, button press / hover timing
and standard easing from Sources/HUDDesignTokens.swift and HUDDesignButtons.swift.
Motion also adapts HUDDesignMotion.swift, HUDSubsectionTransition.swift, and the
ambient timing and excursions in HUDMechanicalArtwork.swift / SystemHUDView.swift.
Native Swift/Core Animation effects are translated to CSS for the Vue interface.
No game artwork, extracted Watch resources, third-party fonts or shaders are bundled.
